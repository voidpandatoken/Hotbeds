How Peak & High-Demand Dates Work

Unlike static coupon codes that fail during blackouts, our desk sources directly from multiple Canadian hotel brands simultaneously. When your dates overlap a major tournament, concert, or convention:

1. Standard Shoulder Nights: Historically achieve ~15% average wholesale savings below retail Best Available Rates.
2. High-Occupancy / Compression Nights: When primary downtown hotels reach 90%+ capacity, we source adjacent transit-corridor properties (e.g., Burlington/Oakville for Hamilton/Toronto, or Canmore for Banff) to protect your budget.
