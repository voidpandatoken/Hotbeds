1.1 Pre-Contractual Disclosure Mandate (Section 4 of Alta. Reg. 81/2001)

Prior to entering into an Internet sales contract on Hotbeds.ca, the consumer is expressly provided with:

  • Itemized Price & Currency Breakdown: A clear, detailed description of the hotel accommodation, room category, occupancy, check-in/check-out dates, base nightly rate in Canadian Dollars (CAD $), applicable federal and provincial sales taxes (GST, PST, HST, QST), and any applicable region-specific fees (such as the mandatory $2.25 CAD/night Ontario Eco Fee for Ontario properties only).
  • Total Contract Obligation: The exact total amount payable by the consumer in Canadian Dollars (CAD $), with zero hidden surcharges, undisclosed resort markups, or post-checkout platform booking fees.
  • Cancellation, Return, Exchange & Refund Restrictions: Full disclosure of whether the selected tariff is a Standard Refundable Rate (eligible for free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to 3:00 PM property local time on the scheduled date of arrival) or a deeply discounted Non-Refundable Flash Rate.
  • Express Opportunity to Accept, Decline, or Correct Errors: At `checkout.html`, consumers are presented with a complete summary of their reservation payload and may modify guest names, dates, province selection, or promotional opt-ins before clicking the final payment submission button.
  • Permanent Copy Retention: Consumers may print or save these disclosures and their completed reservation voucher as a PDF at any time using the "Print / Save Legal Disclosures (PDF)" button at the top of this page or on their booking confirmation screen.

1.2 Statutory 15-Day Contract Delivery & 30-Day Cancellation Rights (Sections 5 & 6)

Under Sections 5 and 6 of the Alberta Internet Sales Contract Regulation (Alta. Reg. 81/2001), consumers are protected by specific statutory cancellation rights in addition to the rate-specific cancellation window displayed at checkout:

  • 15-Day Written Contract Copy Rule (Sec. 5): Hotbeds Inc. delivers a complete, printable electronic copy of the executed Internet sales contract (including your Booking Reference ID, itemized CAD receipt, and hotel voucher) immediately upon payment authorization and to your designated email address within 15 days after the contract is entered into.
  • Statutory Cancellation for Non-Disclosure or Late Copy Delivery (Sec. 6(1)): A consumer may cancel an Internet sales contract at any time from the date the contract is entered into until 7 days after the consumer receives a copy of the contract if Hotbeds Inc. failed to provide the mandatory Section 4 pre-contract disclosures or failed to provide an express opportunity to accept, decline, or correct errors immediately before entering into the contract. Furthermore, a consumer may cancel the contract within 30 days from the date the contract is entered into if Hotbeds Inc. does not provide a copy of the contract within 15 days after the transaction date.
  • Statutory Cancellation for Non-Delivery of Services (Sec. 6(2)): A consumer may cancel an Internet sales contract at any time before the accommodation service is provided if the contracted hotel accommodation is not made available within 30 days of the stated check-in date (or an amended check-in date agreed to in writing by the consumer).
  • Effect of Statutory Cancellation & 15-Day Refund Mandate (Sec. 7 & 8): Notice of statutory cancellation may be delivered to support@hotbeds.ca. Upon valid statutory cancellation under the Alberta CPA, Hotbeds Inc. will refund 100% of all money paid by the consumer within 15 days after the notice of cancellation is given, and if a credit card was used, will process a full credit card reversal upon request in accordance with Section 8 of the Regulation.
Section 02 • Commercial Booking Agreement

Master Reservation Terms, RateHawk Supplier Rules & Cancellation Policy

RateHawk / ETG B2B Pass-Through

2.1 Live Flash Rate Locks (15-Second Feed & 14:59 Checkout Hold)

Hotbeds.ca operates a dynamic flash-rate pricing engine that polls wholesale hotel inventory in real time:

  • 15-Second Live Rate Feed: On our homepage and search feeds, live flash rates cycle on a 15-second market timer with dynamic demand ticks every 3 seconds based on active concurrent property sessions.
  • Locking a Rate & 14:59 Checkout Protection: When a user clicks "Lock In This Rate Now", the current nightly rate (`locked_hotel_price`), property name (`locked_hotel_name`), and provincial tax jurisdiction (`locked_hotel_province`) are frozen in browser session storage and held for 14 minutes and 59 seconds (14:59) on `checkout.html`. If checkout is not completed before the 14:59 timer expires, the temporary inventory allocation is released back to the wholesale pool and pricing reverts to current market availability.

2.2 Cancellation Policy: Refundable vs. Non-Refundable Flash Rates

Standard Refundable Rates

Free Cancellation up to 24h Prior

Reservations booked under our Standard Refundable tariff may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours prior to 3:00 PM (hotel local time) on the scheduled check-in date via `manage-booking.html` or by emailing `support@hotbeds.ca`. Cancellations requested within 24 hours of 3:00 PM check-in incur a penalty equal to the first night's room rate plus applicable taxes.

Non-Refundable Flash Deals

100% Wholesale Commitment

Clearly designated Non-Refundable Flash Rates and promotional distressed-inventory blocks are contracted at deep wholesale discounts. Except where statutory cancellation rights apply under the Alberta CPA (Section 1.2 above), Non-Refundable bookings cannot be cancelled, modified, or refunded once confirmed.

2.3 Front Desk Check-In Requirements & Property Incidentals

All guests checking into a partner hotel booked through Hotbeds.ca must comply with standard Canadian hospitality reception protocols:

  • Government-Issued Photo ID: The primary registered guest named on the reservation must be present at check-in and present a valid, current government-issued photo identification (Driver's Licence, Passport, or Provincial Photo ID Card) matching the booking record.
  • Minimum Check-In Age (18 / 21): The primary guest must be at least 18 years of age (or 19 to 21 years of age at properties containing licensed mini-bars, casino attachments, or provincial liquor restrictions).
  • Credit or Debit Card for Incidentals Deposit: While your room rate and taxes are prepaid through Hotbeds.ca, the hotel front desk will require a valid major credit card (or eligible Visa/Mastercard debit card, subject to property policy) in the primary guest's name upon arrival to place a temporary pre-authorization hold (typically $50–$250 CAD) for incidental charges such as parking, room service, pet fees, or property damage.

2.4 RateHawk / Emerging Travel Group (ETG) B2B Wholesale Supplier Pass-Through Rules

Hotbeds Inc. sources hotel room inventory through direct property contracts and wholesale B2B distribution APIs, including RateHawk (operated by Emerging Travel Group / ETG). By completing a reservation, you agree to the following mandatory supplier pass-through rules:

  • Meal Plan Codes: Room tariffs are governed by standardized international meal codes shown at checkout: RO (Room Only — no meals included), BB (Bed & Breakfast for registered adult occupancy), HB (Half Board), or AI (All-Inclusive). Unconsumed meals cannot be refunded or exchanged for property credit.
  • No-Show Policy: Standard check-in opens at 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM local property time. If a guest fails to arrive before 11:59 PM on the scheduled check-in date without prior notice to `support@hotbeds.ca` or the hotel front desk, the supplier classifies the booking as a No-Show, cancels the remaining nights of the reservation to prevent room spoilage, and retains 100% of the first night's charge (or 100% of the stay total on Non-Refundable tariffs).
  • Bedding & Special Requests: Special requests (e.g., high floor, early check-in, adjacent rooms, or king vs. two queen bedding on "Run of House" categories) are transmitted via the RateHawk API to the property front desk but remain subject to on-site operational availability at check-in.
  • Relocation ("Walk") Protection: In the rare event a supplier property is overbooked or experiences an unforeseen maintenance outage, Hotbeds Inc. and its wholesale supply partner will coordinate comparable or higher-tier alternate accommodation in the same municipal hub at no additional cost to the guest, or provide an immediate 100% refund at the consumer's election.
Section 03 • Nationwide Tax & Fee Transparency

Provincial Tax Schedule & Ontario-Only Eco Fee Disclosure

Strict Regional Isolation

Hotbeds.ca calculates taxes and environmental levies strictly according to the Canadian province in which the booked hotel property is physically located. In compliance with Canadian truth-in-pricing laws and the Alberta CPA, the $2.25 CAD/night ($2.00 base + tax) Ontario Eco Fee is strictly isolated to hotel stays inside the Province of Ontario (`ON`) and is never charged on stays in Alberta, British Columbia, Quebec, the Prairies, or Atlantic Canada.

Province / Region Code Applicable Statutory Tax Rate Tax Breakdown Ontario Eco Fee Handling
Ontario (ON) 13% HST Harmonized Sales Tax (5% Federal + 8% Provincial) $2.25 CAD / night ($2.00 + tax) Mandatory Ontario Eco Fee
Alberta (AB) 5% GST Only 5% Federal GST (0% Provincial Sales Tax) $0.00 Ontario Eco Fee (Exempt)
British Columbia (BC) 13% GST / PST 5% Federal GST + 8% BC Provincial Accommodation PST $0.00 Ontario Eco Fee (Exempt)
Quebec (QC) 15% GST / QST Combined Federal GST & Quebec Sales Tax (QST) $0.00 Ontario Eco Fee (Exempt)

Checkout Calculation Verification: When a guest books a stay in Ontario (such as Hamilton, Burlington, Toronto, Niagara Falls, Mississauga, or Ottawa), the checkout engine applies 13% HST to the room subtotal (after any applicable 20% Guild discount) plus exactly $2.25 CAD per night ($2.00 + $0.25 tax) for the Ontario Eco Fee. When any non-Ontario province (`AB`, `BC`, `QC`, `PR`, `AT`) is selected, the Ontario Eco Fee line item is automatically zeroed out (`$0.00`) and hidden from the charge calculation.
Section 04 • Data Governance & Security

Comprehensive Privacy Policy (PIPEDA & Alberta PIPA Compliant)

PIPEDA & PIPA Certified

Hotbeds Inc. is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of your personal information in accordance with Canada's federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), SA 2003, c P-6.5.

4.1 Categories of Personal Information Collected

We limit the collection of personal information strictly to what is necessary to price, process, and fulfill your hotel reservation, manage your loyalty balance, and provide customer support:

  • Guest Identity & Contact Data: Primary guest full legal name, email address, and mobile/daytime telephone number.
  • Reservation & Stay Metadata: Selected property, city, province, check-in/check-out dates, room occupancy count, special accessibility or bedding notes, and generated Booking Reference ID.
  • Tokenized Payment Credentials (PCI-DSS Level 1): Credit card and debit card payments are processed directly via Stripe (a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 Service Provider). Hotbeds Inc. never stores raw 16-digit primary account numbers (PAN) or CVV security codes on its application servers; only encrypted payment tokens and non-sensitive card brand/last-4-digit confirmation metadata are retained for refund and audit reconciliation.

4.2 Database Architecture & Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS)

All booking records, loyalty point ledgers, and partner applications are stored in encrypted PostgreSQL cloud databases powered by Supabase using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Our database tables enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring that reservation payloads can only be queried by authenticated system functions or verified via matching Booking Reference ID and registered email verification on `manage-booking.html`.

4.3 Strict Purpose Limitation & Third-Party Sharing

Hotbeds Inc. never sells, rents, or trades your personal information to third-party data brokers. Your reservation data is shared exclusively with:

  • The Booked Hotel Property & Wholesale Fulfillment Partner (RateHawk / ETG): Your guest name, stay dates, room type, and contact details are transmitted securely to the specific hotel property you booked (and our wholesale bedbank API) solely to register your front-desk arrival and issue your room key.
  • Payment & Transactional Infrastructure: Stripe (for payment authorization/refunds) and our transactional email gateway (for delivering your statutory contract copy, booking voucher, and requested loyalty credentials).

4.4 Your Statutory Rights: Data Access, Export, Correction & Deletion

Under PIPEDA and Alberta PIPA, you have the right to request a portable export of all personal data held about you, correct inaccurate records, or request permanent deletion/anonymization of your personal profile (subject to statutory Canadian tax and financial audit retention periods). Submit any data access, export, or deletion request to our Privacy Officer at support@hotbeds.ca with the subject line PRIVACY REQUEST; we respond to all verified inquiries within 30 calendar days.
