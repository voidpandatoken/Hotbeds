Corporate & Team FAQ

Frequently Asked Group Questions

Do we receive a single invoice with Canadian GST/HST itemized for our accountant? +

Yes. Hotbeds Inc. is an Alberta-incorporated Canadian company. Every group block invoice is issued in Canadian Dollars (CAD) with provincial occupancy taxes and GST/HST cleanly broken out so your finance team can claim full Input Tax Credits (ITCs).

What if our sports roster or field crew names change before check-in? +

Unlike individual retail bookings where changing a guest name often requires a full cancellation, group blocks allow you to submit and update your master rooming list prior to check-in without losing your locked wholesale rate.

How do our team members access local dining and transit discounts during the stay? +

Once your group block is confirmed, every room receives a complimentary digital Guild Key. Guests simply show their Guild Key on their phone at participating 100% Canadian independent restaurants, bars, and airport transit partners to unlock instant perks.
