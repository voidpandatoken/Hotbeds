Frequently Asked Questions

Hot Beds Club (club.html) is our public-facing Canadian hotel loyalty program: every guest is automatically enrolled for $0.00 on their first booking (or by pre-enrolling on this page) to receive 1,000 Free Welcome Points ($10.00 CAD credit) and earn 10 Points per $1 CAD spent. The Guild is our private Closed User Group (CUG) corporate fraternity and partner network (standard $300/yr membership). When The Guild's promotional opt-in window is active at checkout.html, Hot Beds Club members receive priority eligibility to check the box for free—unlocking a 20% corporate rate reduction plus local city merchant discounts. Existing Guild keyholders can log in anytime at portal-login.html.
Every 100 Hot Beds Club Points convert to $1.00 CAD in hotel stay credit (Points / 100). Your 1,000 Free Welcome Points provide an immediate $10.00 CAD credit, and your ongoing 10 Points per $1 CAD earning rate gives you an uncapped 10% return toward future reservations on Hotbeds.ca.
Hotbeds.ca strictly enforces province-specific tax and fee accuracy. Only Ontario (&prov=ON) properties carry 13% HST plus the mandatory $2.25/night ($2.00 + tax) Ontario Eco Fee at checkout. Properties in British Columbia (&prov=BC), Alberta (&prov=AB), Quebec (&prov=QC), the Prairies (&prov=PR), and Atlantic Canada (&prov=AT) carry $0.00 Eco Fees.
You can view your active hotel reservations and live Hot Beds Club points balance anytime at manage-booking.html. For multi-room corporate blocks (5+ rooms), sports teams, or project crews, visit our Corporate Desk (corporate.html).
